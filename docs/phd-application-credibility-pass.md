# sisfit.cn — PhD Application Credibility Pass

Status: implementation-ready content specification  
Primary objective: make sisfit.cn a credible research portfolio supporting Catherine Ge's PhD applications, without overstating current academic status or converting practice/marketing metrics into research claims.

## 1. Positioning rule

The site should present SISFIT as a **practice-based digital health education platform and research-development context**, not as an established academic research institute unless that status can be independently documented.

The site should present Catherine as a **researcher-in-training / prospective doctoral researcher with substantial practitioner assets**, not as a Principal Investigator unless she is formally PI on a governed research project.

Core narrative:

> Education and large-scale health communication practice → research questions emerging from practice → systematic evidence synthesis → formal research-methods training → doctoral research.

## 9. Evidence ladder

Use this hierarchy when deciding whether a claim appears on the site:

**Level A — externally verifiable**
- DOI
- OSF registration
- published paper
- university transcript/module
- book ISBN/publisher
- institutional event/program page

**Level B — internally documented**
- platform analytics export
- historical content archive
- course/curriculum artifacts
- professional contracts/certificates

**Level C — interpretation / planned research**
- proposed framework
- prospective doctoral question
- future methodology
- hypothesised mechanism

Never write Level C as Level A.
